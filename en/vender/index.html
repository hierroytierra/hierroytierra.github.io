What we commit to do

Five commitments in writing

Your peace of mind comes first: discretion, and visits only with your approval.

Commitment

Discretion

We do not publish the rural district, the ranch name, or your name. Interested parties learn only the region.

Commitment

Visits arranged with you

No interested party learns the location or visits the ranch without your prior approval. Every visit is coordinated with you and accompanied by us.

Commitment

A listing that tells the truth

We visit the ranch and present it with real photographs and aerial footage: water, paddocks, corrals, and houses. We publish only what we have verified.

Commitment

You know where things stand

We report to you on WhatsApp, at an agreed frequency, on how many interested parties there were, which visits took place, and where each negotiation stands.

Commitment

Through to the deed

We coordinate the purchase agreement, the notary, and the livestock documentation together with your attorney and your accountant.

How it works

From the first call to the deed

    We guide you through the negotiation, the purchase agreement, and the deed.

Frequently asked questions

What owners ask us

Will you publish my ranch online?

Not with its exact location or your name. The listing circulates privately; only the region is mentioned.

What is your fee?

A commission on the sale price, plus VAT, payable only if the ranch sells. We give you the percentage on the first call, and it is set out in writing.

How long does a sale take?

We will not promise you a timeline. It depends mainly on the price, the region, and how many buyers there are for that type of ranch.

Do I have to sign an exclusive agreement?

We propose an exclusive listing agreement for a defined term, with an extension only if you sign it. This allows us to invest in the visit, the aerial photography, and the private marketing of your ranch.

Do you buy the ranch yourselves?

We do not currently buy ranches with our own capital. If you need liquidity quickly, we look for a buyer with funds ready, and you decide whether the price works for you.
