How we work

We represent cattle properties in private sales. We present them with real photographs, keep their location private, and support the transaction through to the deed.

If you are buying

The visit first, then the documents

The listing presents the property. The transaction is handled in person, step by step.

  1. The visit

    We walk the entire ranch: paddocks, water, housing, and infrastructure. One day, no obligation.

  2. The documents

    Your attorney reviews the certificate of title and the ranch's documentation.

  3. Purchase agreement and deed

    Purchase agreement signed with the owner, and the deed.

If you are selling

From the first call to the deed

Private sale: your ranch is not published with its location or your name.

  1. First contact

    You message us or fill out the form, and an advisor calls you. It is a no-obligation conversation.

  2. Visit

    We go to the ranch, walk it with you, and gather photographs and details.

  3. Listing and visits

    We prepare the private listing and present it to interested parties. You approve each visit, and we are present at every one.

  4. Closing

    We guide you through the negotiation, the purchase agreement, and the deed.

Discretion

What we publish and what we do not

What we do publish

Municipality and region, hectares, price, water sources, grasses, houses, and corrals, with real, unretouched photos taken on the ranch.

We do not publish

The rural district, the ranch name, coordinates, or the owner's name.

Only what we have verified

Each listing is built from what we saw on the property.

Nothing automated

Every message is answered by a member of our team. We do not use bots to assist buyers or owners.
